Here’s a practical roundup of solid coffee machines you’re likely under €300 in Europe today. Prices vary by country and retailer, and promos can push some models below or above the mark, but these are reliable picks that regularly sit under €300.

Best all‑round espresso machines (semi‑automatic) under €300
- De’Longhi Dedica EC685 (or EC685.EB)
  - Why pick it: ultra‑compact, good espresso and milk frothing for the price, solid build, easy to use.
  - What to know: small water tank and cup height limits; best with a separate milk frother if you want latte art.
  - Typical price: around €180–€240.

- Gaggia Viva Prestige
  - Why pick it: simple, durable, good crema for a non‑PID machine, straightforward maintenance.
  - What to know: basic frothing wand (not a fancy automatic milk system).
  - Typical price: around €199–€249.

Best capsule/pod machines under €300
- Nespresso Essenza Mini
  - Why pick it: tiny footprint, very reliable espresso, simple to use, great for kitchens with limited space.
  - What to know: ongoing cost of capsules; not ideal if you want a long coffee with milk systems built in.
  - Typical price: often €99–€149.

- Nespresso Lattissima One
  - Why pick it: one‑touch milk drinks with a compact footprint; excellent for cappuccinos and lattes at the push of a button.
  - What to know: longer‑term capsule costs; a bit larger than Essenza Mini.
  - Typical price: around €199–€299.

- Nespresso VertuoPlus (where available)
  - Why pick it: can brew different cup sizes (espresso and lungo) and uses a different capsule type.
  - What to know: Vertuo capsules are a separate ecosystem; price varies by model and promotions.
  - Typical price: often €149–€299.

Best drip coffee machines under €300
- Melitta Look Therm or similar Look series
  - Why pick it: simple, easy to use for regular drip coffee, good capacity, affordable.
  - What to know: basic presets; no milk frothing.
  - Typical price: €50–€120.

- Bosch/Therm/Similar budget drip brewers
  - Why pick it: reliable, easy maintenance, good for larger cups.
  - What to know: often basic features; look for auto‑shutoff and auto‑pause if that matters to you.
  - Typical price: €60–€140.

Budget all‑in‑one and value options
- Krups XP3440 or similar budget semi‑automatics
  - Why pick it: very good value for entry‑level espresso making, simple to maintain.
  - What to know: build quality and longevity will be good but not premium.
  - Typical price: €100–€170.

Tips to choose quickly
- If you want espresso and milk drinks with minimal effort: consider a capsule machine like Nespresso Lattissima One.
- If you value drink customization and have time to learn: a compact semi‑automatic like De’Longhi Dedica EC685 or Gaggia Viva Prestige.
- If you drink mostly drip coffee: a simple Melitta or Bosch drip brewer is hard to beat for price and ease.
- If you’re price‑checking: watch for sales around holiday seasons; many models hover near €150–€250 and drop to under €150 during promos.
