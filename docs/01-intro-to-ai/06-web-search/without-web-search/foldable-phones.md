Availability and model lineups differ a lot by country in Asia, but as of now you’ll find several strong foldables that are easy to buy across major markets (India, Southeast Asia, China, Hong Kong, Singapore, Malaysia, etc.). Here are the best current options, with quick notes on what they’re good for and where you’re likely to find them.

Best overall for multitasking and top experience

- Samsung Galaxy Z Fold (latest generation)
  - Why: best all-around folding phone for productivity and media. Large inner screen, solid multitasking, mature software, strong ecosystem.
  - Cons: high price, thicker and heavier than non-foldables.
  - Availability: widely sold across Asia.

Best compact foldable (clamshell)

- Samsung Galaxy Z Flip (latest generation)
  - Why: pocketable form factor, modern tech in a small footprint, great for everyday use.
  - Cons: smaller outer screen and battery life compared to the Fold.
  - Availability: widely sold across Asia.

Top value and camera-focused foldables

- Oppo Find N series (Find N2/N3 or newer)
  - Why: excellent hinge and compact inner display with strong cameras; good value for money versus Samsung’s folds.
  - Availability: strong in China and many Southeast Asian markets; India often gets these via official channels.

- Xiaomi Mix Fold (Mix Fold 4/5 or newer)
  - Why: big inner display, competitive specs, often priced lower than Samsung equivalents.
  - Availability: broad in China and India; sold in several Asian markets.

Other notable Asia-friendly options

- Honor Magic V/V2/V3 (foldable line from Honor)
  - Why: strong build and good value, usually priced below top-tier Samsung models.
  - Availability: heavy in China; India and some SEA markets via online/offline channels.

- Vivo X Fold / X Fold Plus (and newer variants)
  - Why: solid foldables with good cameras and screens; strong regional presence.
  - Availability: widely sold in China and parts of Asia.

- OnePlus Open (and successors)
  - Why: clean OxygenOS software, good performance, competitive pricing for a flagship foldable.
  - Availability: strongest in India and select Southeast Asian markets; check local availability.

- Google Pixel Fold (availability varies)
  - Why: best-in-class software experience and camera reliability in foldable form.
  - Availability: varies by country; sometimes limited to certain markets in Asia (e.g., Singapore, select regions). Check local availability.

Tips to choose

- If you want the best multitasking and a refined folding experience, go Samsung Galaxy Z Fold.
- If you want a pocketable phone with a modern foldable experience at a lower price, consider Z Flip or Oppo Find N series.
- If value per spec matters, Xiaomi Mix Fold or Oppo Find N series often punch above their price.
- If you care about software experience and camera quality, Pixel Fold is compelling where it’s available; otherwise consider OnePlus Open or Honor/Vivo options.
- Availability and prices can vary a lot by country, carrier, and retailer. Local service centers and spare parts support are worth considering too.
