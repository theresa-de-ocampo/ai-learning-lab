Here are strong, well-regarded coffee machines you can usually find under €300 in Europe (as of June 2026). I’ve grouped them by typical use case and included what each is good at, plus typical price ranges.

- De’Longhi Dedica EC685 (or EC685 Style)
  - Why it’s good: A slim, 15 cm-wide manual espresso machine that handles both ground coffee and ESE pods, with a real steam wand for milk drinks. Great all-around value and reliability.
  - Typical price range: about €120–€180 (often ~€130).
  - Notable specs: 15-bar pump, 1.1 L water tank, compact footprint.
  - Sources: Brewmance’s budget espresso guide (spots EC685 as best under €300) and price references; European price data also shows EC685 around €128–€130. ([brewmance.fr](https://www.brewmance.fr/en/comparatif/best-espresso-machine-under-300/))

- Sage (Breville in US) Bambino
  - Why it’s good: A step up in quality with a bigger 54 mm portafilter, quick heat-up (ThermoJet), and strong milk texturing for lattes and cappuccinos. Great “upgrade now” machine under €300 if you pair it with a grinder.
  - Typical price range: roughly €250–€300 (often ~€270).
  - Notable specs: Ground coffee only (no capsule option), 1.9 L reservoir, fast heat-up.
  - Sources: Brewmance lists Sage Bambino at about €270; other EU price listings for Bambino around €288–€290. ([brewmance.fr](https://www.brewmance.fr/en/comparatif/best-espresso-machine-under-300/))

- Krups Nespresso Pixie (XN306/T)
  - Why it’s good: Ultra-compact capsule machine with quick heat-up and consistent espresso from capsules. If you want zero-fuss, this is hard to beat for size and speed.
  - Typical price range: about €100–€150 (often around €100–€120).
  - Notable specs: 19-bar pump, 0.7 L water tank; capsule-based (no grind or milk foaming built-in).
  - Source: Brewmance capsule-machine shortlist (Pixie listed around €100). ([brewmance.fr](https://www.brewmance.fr/en/comparatif/best-espresso-machine-under-300/))

- Nespresso Inissia De’Longhi
  - Why it’s good: The cheapest “real espresso” you can buy; extremely simple to use with consistent results from Original capsules.
  - Typical price range: around €70–€90 (often ~€80).
  - Notable specs: 19-bar pump, 0.7 L tank; capsule-only.
  - Source: Brewmance listing for Inissia under €100. ([brewmance.fr](https://www.brewmance.fr/en/comparatif/best-espresso-machine-under-300/))

- De’Longhi EC235 (or similar ground-coffee entry model)
  - Why it’s good: A very affordable way to use real ground coffee with a steam wand; basic but functional.
  - Typical price range: about €50–€70.
  - Notable specs: 15-bar pump, 1.0 L tank; accepts ground coffee or ESE pods.
  - Source: Brewmance lists EC235 as the budget ground-coffee option under €300. ([brewmance.fr](https://www.brewmance.fr/en/comparatif/best-espresso-machine-under-300/))
